About

How I think about design, and where I focus my time.

I'm a professional web developer working across UI/UX design, branding, and full front-end build — from business websites to complete e-commerce stores, I take a project from a blank page to a working, polished site without losing the original idea along the way.

My approach stays close to the content: typography, spacing, and colour choices come from what a project actually needs, not from a template. I care about pages that load fast, work on every screen, and feel calm to use.
